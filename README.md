Ethical Finder
Ethical Finder is a web application designed to help consumers evaluate brands based on their environmental impact, animal welfare standards, and labor practices. Developed as a first-year multidisciplinary project at VIT Chennai, the platform presents standardized ratings for over 50 consumer brands to promote transparent and conscious purchasing decisions.

Live Links
Website: https://ethicalfinder.vercel.app/

GitHub Repository: https://github.com/aaaryann24/ethicalfinder

Features
Brand Ratings: Evaluations for 50+ fashion and lifestyle brands.

Environmental Impact Analysis: Assessment of carbon usage, material sustainability, and waste management.

Animal Welfare Criteria: Verification of cruelty-free certifications and humane policies.

Labor and Ethical Practice Scores: Evaluation of supply chain transparency, fair wages, and worker safety.

Research-Backed Framework: Rating scores derived from academic research papers and community feedback.

Rating Methodology
Brands are evaluated across three primary categories:

Environmental Friendliness: Focuses on energy efficiency, chemical policies, sustainable sourcing, and waste reduction.

Animal Welfare: Tracks animal testing practices, cruelty-free certifications, and the use of animal products.

Ethical Values: Examines workplace safety, fair living wages, and supply chain accountability.
